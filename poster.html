Sensor Fusion Driving

A camera and a 19-number state vector learn to steer from an expert. Reinforcement learning then keeps the car on the road much longer, and still never reaches the destination.

EfficientNet-B0PPOMetaDrive

Question

  • Why does a low imitation loss still drive off the road, and what does fine-tuning change?

Method

  • EfficientNet-B0 on a 128×128 frame, an MLP on 19 state values, fused to steering and acceleration.
  • Behavioral cloning on IDM demonstrations, then PPO warm-started from the sensor branch.
  • Grad-CAM on the turns the imitator misses.

Evidence

0.00043best validation MSE
+655%survival vs the imitator, at 480k steps
0%success at a destination

What the result actually says

  • Best imitation loss is 0.00043 MSE at epoch 20. The network is 4.89 million parameters, 882 thousand of them trainable while the backbone is frozen.
  • On 20 unseen episodes the imitator arrives nowhere: 19 run off the road, 1 crashes, average 299 steps and about 98 meters. Grad-CAM shows moderate turns attending to sky and terrain.
  • PPO warm-started from the sensor branch. At step 480,000 the average episode is 2,256 steps, about 7.5 times the imitator. Destination success stays 0%.

What it does not show

  • The +655% figure is survival time, not trips completed. The +3000 destination bonus is never collected in 500,000 steps.
  • Crash rate is 0% at the end, after a 5–10% spike around steps 280,000–310,000. Out-of-road is still about 90% at the long-survival checkpoint.
  • Checkpoints, the driving logs, and both datasets stay on this machine. GitHub has the code and the poster.

Beside the other attempts

  • Camera-only validation loss in the published comparison is 0.05322. The fusion number used for the stated 81.5% gain was 0.00985. The saved fusion checkpoint later reached 0.00043.
  • Extreme turns are predicted closely. The failure is the moderate steering band, about 0.1 to 0.4, which the loss under-weights.

Stack

EfficientNet-B0, a 19-d sensor MLP, MetaDrive, Stable-Baselines3 PPO. The dashboard environment is car_env. The checkpoint is checkpoints_v2/best_model.pth.
