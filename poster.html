Sensor Fusion Driving

A camera and a 19-number state learn to copy an expert. Reinforcement learning then keeps the car up much longer. Finishing the route is the open goal.

EfficientNet-B0PPOMetaDrive
  1. 01 · Watch an expertIDM demonstrations: a 128×128 frame plus 19 state numbers, and the action that was taken.
  2. 02 · Copy itEfficientNet on the image, a small MLP on the state, fused into steering and acceleration.
  3. 03 · See the missGrad-CAM on the turns the imitator gets wrong. Moderate steering is the band the loss under-weights.
  4. 04 · Drive longerPPO warm-started from the sensor branch, so it does not begin from a random policy.

Question

  • Why does a tiny imitation loss still leave the road, and what does a second phase of learning change?

Why this path

  • Camera-only was the baseline. Adding the 19 state values is what dropped the error.
  • Imitation learns the average action. The rare moderate turn is where the car leaves the lane, so a second phase has to practice driving, not just copying.

What held

0.00043best validation MSE, epoch 20
~7.5×longer episodes after PPO, vs the imitator
19 + imagestate vector fused with the camera

Reading the result

  • The network is 4.89 million parameters. 882 thousand train while the backbone is frozen, then the backbone is unfrozen and the loss keeps falling.
  • The imitator, on 20 unseen episodes, runs off the road. Average life is about 300 steps and about 98 meters. Extreme turns are predicted closely. The miss is the gentle bend.
  • After PPO the average episode is much longer, on the order of a couple of thousand steps. The car survives. Reaching the destination is the target the reward has not collected yet.

Why the loss and the road disagree

  • Most training frames are easy: straight, or a sharp turn the expert takes often. A small error there dominates the average.
  • Grad-CAM on the missed turns looks at sky and terrain. The fusion was built so the state vector can cover what the image attends to poorly.

The comparison that mattered

  • Camera-only validation loss in the published check is about 0.053. Fusion is far below that. The saved checkpoint reached 0.00043.
